Privacy Policy

Story Unfolded Poster · Last updated: 28 October 2026

Story Unfolded Poster ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the Story Unfolded YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

1. Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This allows the app to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and related details, to the YouTube channel of the signed-in account. The app does not request access to any other Google data.

2. How the data is used

The access is used only to upload videos that the channel owner chooses to publish. The app does not view, analyse, modify or delete any other content on your YouTube account.

3. Storage

The OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored only on the device where the app runs, so it can upload without asking you to sign in every time. Video files are read from that device and sent directly to YouTube. The app does not run its own servers and does not keep copies of your data elsewhere.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as needed to perform the upload.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's files from your device removes any stored tokens.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
