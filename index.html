Story Unfolded Poster

A private upload tool for the Story Unfolded YouTube channel

What this app does

Story Unfolded Poster is a small, private tool used by the owner of the Story Unfolded YouTube channel to upload their own videos to that channel using the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it accesses

The app asks for one Google permission only: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account that signs in. It does not read, edit or delete other YouTube data, and it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

Who can use it

This app is not a public service. It is used only by the channel owner to post content to their own channel.
